## The active thread: groomed-ρ normalization window study

Aritra is studying restricting the ρ data/MC comparison to the measured core:
window cuts at log(ρ²) > −3.5 and > −3.0, normalization inside the window.
Rendered for 2018, both channels, all pT bins, both cuts, in
`campaign_2018/rho_window_study/`. Findings so far:
- Windowed normalization shrinks the core shape bands substantially: dijet
  medians 5–10% → 1.9–3.4%, trijet → 1.9–4.8%; FSR dominant everywhere
  (2.8–5.2%), then ISR, JMS.
- The residual disagreement is a coherent tilt: data ~10% above MC on the soft
  shoulder (−2.5 to −2), ~10% below at the peak (−1 to −0.7).
- Full-window vs windowed scale factor: ×1.09 vs ×1.13 (dijet 200–290) — the
  soft shoulder pulls the normalization down.
Likely next tests he may ask for: a finer window scan, the same windows on the
other eras or on ungroomed ρ (`ptjet_rhojet_u_reco`, same function, `ftag`
`rho_u`), or an m_SD analogue. Rendering pattern:
